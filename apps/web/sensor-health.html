What is AirSense and What Does It Do?

A simple, non-technical overview for students, faculty, and campus visitors

CAMPUS AIR MONITORING: AirSense is a physical weather station installed on campus measuring the actual air you are breathing right now in Karachi.
WHAT SENSORS MEASURE: A laser measures dust, smoke, and smog (PM2.5/PM10). Built-in sensors measure temperature, humidity, and atmospheric pressure, while a rain sensor detects raindrops immediately.
WHY GROUND-TRUTH MATTERS: Instead of delayed city estimates from distant airports, AirSense delivers second-by-second ground truth data so students and faculty know when outdoor air is safe.
