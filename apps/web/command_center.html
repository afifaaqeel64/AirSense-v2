What is AirSense and What Does It Do?

A simple, non-technical overview for students, faculty, and campus visitors

Real-Time Campus Air Monitoring: AirSense is a physical weather and environmental monitoring station deployed on campus. Just like checking the weather on your phone, AirSense measures the actual air you are breathing right now in Karachi.
What the Sensors Measure: A precision laser sensor counts dust, smoke, and smog particles (PM2.5 & PM10). Built-in sensors measure temperature, humidity, and barometric pressure, while a rain sensor detects precipitation immediately.
Why Ground-Truth Data Matters: Standard weather apps rely on forecasts from distant airports hours late. AirSense delivers second-by-second ground truth data so you know when outdoor sports are healthy or when sensitive individuals should stay indoors.
